Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

AdBlockAll · Last updated: October 22, 2026

English

AdBlockAll is built to work without collecting anything from you.

What the app does

AdBlockAll blocks advertising, tracking and analytics domains on your iPhone in two ways:

  1. Network-wide blocking — the app installs a DNS proxy (an iOS system extension). When any app on your phone looks up a domain, the extension compares it against blocking rules stored on the device and refuses to answer if it matches. This happens entirely on your device.
  2. Safari blocking — the app provides a content blocker that Safari uses to hide or stop ad elements on web pages.

What we collect

Nothing. Specifically:

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.
  • The DNS queries your device makes are processed on the device by the system extension. They are never sent to us or to any server we operate.
  • The app has no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash-reporting service, and no tracking of any kind.
  • Blocking statistics shown inside the app are computed and stored locally on your device and never leave it.

Network requests the app makes

The app itself only contacts the following, at your request:

  • Rule subscription sources (for example EasyList, EasyList China, EasyPrivacy) — public rule lists downloaded over HTTPS when you tap “Update” or on a periodic refresh you control. Your device downloads these lists directly from the public servers that publish them; no personal data is included in or added to these requests.
  • Domains you add to your own custom subscription sources.

Data storage

Rules, settings, and statistics are stored in the app’s local storage and App Group container on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of them.

Children

The app contains no content directed at children and collects no data from any user, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this URL with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/BigBadRock/adblock.

中文

AdBlockAll 的设计原则是：不向你收集任何东西。

本应用做什么

AdBlockAll 通过两种方式在你的 iPhone 上拦截广告、追踪与统计域名：

  1. 全网拦截 —— 应用安装一个 DNS 代理（iOS 系统扩展）。手机上任何应用查询域名时， 扩展会用保存在设备上的规则做比对，命中则不予应答。整个过程完全在你的设备上完成。
  2. Safari 拦截 —— 应用提供内容拦截器，供 Safari 用来隐藏或阻止网页上的广告元素。

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。 具体来说：

  • 我们不收集、不存储、不传输任何个人数据。
  • 你设备发出的 DNS 查询由系统扩展在设备本地处理，永远不会发给我们或我们运营的任何服务器。
  • 应用不含分析 SDK、广告 SDK、崩溃上报服务，也没有任何形式的追踪。
  • 应用内显示的拦截统计在设备本地计算和保存，从不离开你的设备。

应用会访问的网络

应用本身只在你主动操作时访问以下内容：

  • 规则订阅源（例如 EasyList、EasyList China、EasyPrivacy）—— 当你点击「更新」或在你控制的 定期刷新时，通过 HTTPS 下载公开规则列表。你的设备直接从发布这些列表的公共服务器下载；这些请求不包含、 也不添加任何个人数据。
  • 你自己添加的自定义订阅源的域名。

数据存储

规则、设置和统计保存在你设备上的应用本地存储与 App Group 容器中。删除应用即删除全部数据。

儿童

应用不含面向儿童的内容，也不收集任何用户（包括儿童）的数据。

政策变更

若本政策发生变化，新版本将发布在本页面并更新日期。

联系我们
